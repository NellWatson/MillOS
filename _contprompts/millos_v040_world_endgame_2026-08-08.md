# MillOS v0.40 Operational World Endgame

## Product decision

Deepen the accepted continuous world in place as an uncrewed autonomous mill. Preserve every authored district and industrial system while removing all runtime human presence. Improve spatial truth, non-human activity, logistics, industrial art, atmosphere, SCADA, interface clarity, startup, and frame pacing without changing the `0.40.0` identity.

## Adversarial pre-check

1. PR #6 is a clean, green, reviewable exact candidate, but its fresh headed runtime proof is blocked by unrelated automation-browser ownership. New work must not contaminate that baseline.
2. A Cartesian capture of every quality, weather, time, and camera combination would create more than a thousand frames and thermally invalidate performance evidence. Use full coverage on Medium plus pairwise representative coverage for the other dimensions.
3. Existing world, logistics, SCADA, weather, and water systems are mature. Extend their authorities and contracts rather than add parallel stores or replacement scenes.
4. Performance changes require trace evidence. Large-file decomposition, batching, worker offload, and lazy boundaries are conditional remedies, not automatic goals.
5. Spatial fixes can vandalize the authored composition when inferred from source coordinates alone. Footprints, routes, Blender harnesses, fixed cameras, and running-scene inspection are the joint authority.
6. The user explicitly requires no humans at all. Personnel models, vehicle occupants, remote avatars, human voice ambience, worker simulation hooks, personnel review cameras, selection routes, workforce panels, fire-drill controls, and worker-generated interface activity must be absent from runtime.

## Execution order

1. Preserve PR #6 and close its exact-commit browser acceptance when the automation lane is free.
2. Establish spatial footprint, clearance, route, and depth regression contracts.
3. Deepen non-human world activity and weather-driven water and drainage.
4. Finish vehicle, dock, cargo, gate, light, audio, and exception choreography.
5. Refine industrial models, process legibility, materials, shaders, lighting, and surface stability.
6. Advance atmosphere, celestial state, cameras, acoustic zones, SCADA, operator UX, writing, accessibility, and responsive behavior.
7. Optimise cold start, draw calls, updates, assets, and bundle boundaries only where measurements show a material constraint.
8. Run the representative acceptance matrix, update evidence, publish the stacked review branch, and keep the version locked.

## Final machine evidence

- The version lock passed across package, lockfile root, and lockfile package metadata at exactly `0.40.0`.
- The final unit aggregate passed 108 files and 1,696 tests with one worker. The additional regression proves CSV export uses the parallel partial-result historian path.
- The production build passed with 3,681 modules. Initial JavaScript is 0.61 MiB gzip and the complete build is 99.36 MiB.
- Typecheck, lint, full format check, diff check, asset validation, depth validation, shader validation, bundle validation, and Impeccable design lint passed. Depth coverage includes 55 active files and 12 resolved relationships; shader coverage includes 22 families and 22 stable cache keys.
- The exhaustive desktop and mobile Playwright application path passed in Chromium. It covered SCADA trends and CSV export, simulated faults, alarm acknowledgement, autonomous emergency-stop controls, settings, Low-to-Medium quality switching, diagnostic export, keyboard focus, reduced motion, mobile dialogs, and WCAG A/AA scans with no console, page, or request errors.
- All 19 authored cameras captured the continuously mounted world with zero worker-store population and zero matching scene objects. The final farm and logistics captures supersede the pre-LOD farm frame and preserve the authored town, stream, garage, maintenance district, factory shell, windows, yards, mountain ring, sky, sun, and moon.
- The dedicated midnight run passed at 67.27 FPS for overview and 119.97 FPS for moon, with the procedural moon, stars, sky, and mountain backdrop visibly wired to the canonical clock.
- The authoritative non-art Medium run passed every targeted render-cost gate at DPR 1.2: water 105.5 FPS and 10.5 ms p95, farm 97.2 FPS and 11.5 ms p95, shipping 119.2 FPS and 9.7 ms p95, logistics-close 120.0 FPS and 9.8 ms p95. Every sample preserved the world contract and reported zero humans.
- Startup passed at 303.2 ms natively and 4,118.4 ms under Fast 3G, both below the 4-second and 8-second limits.
- Motion telemetry proves forklift travel, truck phase and travel, wheel rotation, and trailer articulation. The source contract excludes worker mounts, vehicle occupants, remote avatars, human voice producers, workforce UI, personnel cameras, fire-drill controls, and the farm scarecrow.
